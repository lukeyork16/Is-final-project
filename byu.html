Why I Love It

Saturdays in Provo are hard to beat. The whole town turns blue, everybody is walking to LaVell Edwards Stadium, and you can see the Y on the mountain from just about anywhere. I've been a fan for as long as I can remember, and being a student here now makes it even better.

BYU started playing college football in 1922 and won the national championship in 1984. They have won 23 conference titles and officially joined the Big 12 on July 1, 2023. Not bad for a school a lot of people still think of as an underdog.

My Favorite BYU Seasons

  1. 1984
    • Went 13 and 0 under LaVell Edwards
    • Beat Michigan in the Holiday Bowl
    • National champions
  2. 1996
    • 14 wins, which set an NCAA record at the time
    • Won the Cotton Bowl
    • Finished ranked No. 5
  3. 2025
    • Went 12 and 2
    • Played in the Big 12 Championship Game
    • Beat Georgia Tech in the Pop-Tarts Bowl

Highlights: BYU 41, UCF 21 (2025)

One of my favorite games from last season. The Cougars handled UCF at home to finish the regular season strong.
